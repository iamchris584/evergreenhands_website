Evergreen Hands Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us when using our website forms. This includes:

  • Contact Form: Name, email address, phone number, and message contents.
  • Donation Form: Name, email address, and billing information (processed directly by Stripe).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your contact form questions or support requests.
  • Issue donation receipts and processing confirmations.
  • Maintain operational security and prevent fraudulent transactions.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only with trusted service infrastructure partners such as our payment gateway (Stripe) strictly to complete transactions.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement standard industry security controls to protect your data. Your contact details are stored securely for as long as necessary to manage our organizational communication or comply with legal requirements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or ask us to delete your records. To make a request, contact us at info@evergreenhands.org.
