Our Core Pillars of Impact

  • Sickle Cell Disease Support: Advocacy, peer connection, and help accessing better, more joined-up care working alongside NHS services already in place across Scotland.
  • Poverty Relief & Refugee Support: Direct support for people facing financial hardship, with particular focus on asylum seekers and refugees living with Sickle Cell Disease, who often face both health and financial barriers at once.
