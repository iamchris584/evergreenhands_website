Evergreen Hands Donation Refund Policy

1. General Policy

All donations made to Evergreen Hands are considered voluntary, non-refundable contributions. Because these funds are immediately allocated to support our ongoing community programs and operations, we are generally unable to process refunds once a donation is completed.

2. Exceptions & Errors

If you believe an error was made during your transaction (such as an accidental duplicate charge or an incorrect donation amount typed), please contact us immediately at info@evergreenhands.org.

3. Refund Requests

We review accidental transaction errors on a case-by-case basis. Refund requests must be submitted in writing within 14 days of the original transaction date. Approved refunds will be credited back to the original credit card or payment method used for the donation. Please note that processing times may vary depending on your financial institution.
